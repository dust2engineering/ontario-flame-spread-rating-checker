OBC 2024 Subsection 3.1.13 — maximum flame-spread ratings for interior finishes, corridors and high buildings.

Pick a location and click Check.

Rules designers miss

Doors (outside Group A Div. 1) escape Table 3.1.13.2 if their FSR is ≤ 200; doors inside a dwelling unit are exempt. Up to 10% of a wall or ceiling that must be under 150 may still be 150 (25% of wall area in 3.4.4.2.(2) lobbies). Light diffusers may reach FSR 250 with a smoke developed classification ≤ 600. Bathrooms in residential suites: FSR ≤ 200. And outside high buildings, Subsection 3.1.13 has no general smoke table — SDC limits live in specific articles only.
